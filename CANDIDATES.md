# Warning Label Candidates

1. WARNING: May contain traces of other humans' opinions. Handle with extreme prejudice against your own logic.

2. CAUTION: Contains 70% water and 100% nonsense. Keep away from important decisions.

3. DANGER: Highly susceptible to FOMO and bad life choices. Not responsible for imp tattoos or cryptocurrency investments.

4. WARNING: Side effects include overconfidence, poor sleep decisions, and questionable fashion choices. User assumes all risks.

5. CAUTION: May spontaneously combust at 3 AM while worrying about things that won't matter tomorrow.

## Chosen

#4. WARNING: Side effects include overconfidence, poor sleep decisions, and questionable fashion choices. User assumes all risks.

This wins because it reads like an actual pharmaceutical warning label, hits on universal human experiences (overconfidence, poor sleep, bad fashion), has authentic warning wording, and delivers a relatable laugh about our own poor decisions.
